around a month ago, i purchased a **2000 Land Rover Discovery 2.** if you thought
that the Z3 was a horrific financial decision, just wait until you get a load of this.

## original condition
the car came with a host of different issues on purchase.
- misfire on cylinder whatever.
- rear right O2 sensor had its wire devoured by the driveshaft.
- a bunch of other sensors also were curiously showing 0 activity
- front brakes are on the out
- front shocks are on the out
- left low beam headlight was out
- no bumper lol
- ac doesn't blow, but is cold
- couldn't really turn signal properly
- trunk door doesn't exactly enjoy opening

## solutions
a lot of the issues have been fixed in the last month.

### misfire
the misfire was the main time-sensitive problem, in that it would probably be inadvisable to
drive for long distances while the car was missing so hard. it was causing a flashing CEL, meaning
enough unburned fuel must have gone through to start registering damage to the cats. after (improperly)
doing the spark plugs to little avail, and having a shop do a compression test, we generally isolated
the issue to either the ignition coils/wires or the fuel injectors. 

the previous owner decided to run the AutoZone special spark plug wires, meaning those were a highly
likely candidate for causing a miss. and lo and behold, when we were unplugging the wires, one of the connectors
had developed a crack and stayed inside the ignition coil. upon replacement of the coils and wires, we were 
fortunately firing on all cylinders again. otherwise, i would have been livid.

### o2s
the other miscellaneous sensors were not the main cause of the misfire, but did exacerbate the symptoms.
the reason a bunch of sensors were dead was because whatever took out the bottom right O2 sensor also
got the attached fuse, which killed everything else. very simple fix that kinda freaked me out.

the front left O2 sensor was installed with the care and delicacy of a sledgehammer. i'm not sure how they
managed to completely mutilate the male-end connector (the one that's extremely hard to replace!!!), but
i ended up making it much worse by deciding to splice a new set of wires on instead of re-pinning the original. 
i'm not gonna lie, i just had my friend do it and made him dinner as repayment. but it runs pretty well now!

### other misc improvements
also purchased a new bumper (and kinda busted my finger trying to get it on) and replaced the tail lights, which
has significantly improved the cosmetic state of the car. i will not talk about the lack of windshield washer 
fluid, as that seems like an issue for another time

## tl;dr
after buying a pretty rough car and owning it for a month and putting quite a bit of money into it, it is now
slightly better! granted, the suspension and brakes are still pending (brakes happening this weekend, suspension
whenever i get my psc bonus). there is also still some wiring with the knock sensor that MIGHT have gotten 
slightly pinched when we did the ignition coils.

but the car runs now. i wouldn't say well just yet, but it is in a significantly better shape than when i bought 
it. take that, haters.
